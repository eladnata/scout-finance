Investigative Audit

Focused investigative audit support when management needs facts, structure and professional independence around a sensitive financial or operational concern.

When this service is typically relevant

  • When an unusual event, pattern or concern requires independent examination.
  • When management needs a structured fact base before deciding on next steps.
  • When international or subsidiary activity requires focused investigative review.

Typical scope

  • Investigative audit
  • Focused financial review
  • Process and evidence examination
  • International investigative audit support

What the work can include

  • Define the question and scope carefully
  • Preserve a fact-based and independent approach
  • Review relevant records, processes and evidence
  • Identify inconsistencies and control implications
  • Report findings clearly to the appropriate governance level

The scope is tailored to the organization, the governance need and the operating environment. The purpose is to create a usable management view — not additional complexity.
