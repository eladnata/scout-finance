About Scout Finance

Scout Finance Management Consulting & Audit is an Israeli firm specializing in accountancy, internal and external audit, consulting and risk management. Founded in 2015, the firm brings together accountants, economists, computer specialists and articled clerks.

A firm built around professional judgement

Scout Finance provides accountancy, internal and external audit, consulting and risk-management services to government offices, institutions, companies, public organizations and nonprofits with complex financial activity.

The firm was founded in 2015. Its professional staff includes accountants, economists, computer specialists and articled clerks, combining financial and operational perspectives around each assignment.

The firm’s work spans Israel and international operations, with stated experience in Germany, Luxembourg, Canada, the United States, China and Japan.

Leadership

Scout Finance’s advisory team is led by Tomer Natan (LLM, BA, CPA), with more than 25 years of experience across technology, finance and audit. His background includes CFO roles, board and audit-committee experience, biotechnology and R&D financial management, and an early career at Ernst & Young Israel.
